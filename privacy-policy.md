# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 4, 2026_

This policy covers the personal video upload tool ("the App") run by its owner (GitHub: abdyzam50-rgb).

**What the App receives from TikTok.** When you connect, TikTok gives the App your account's open ID, basic profile info (display name and avatar), and access tokens that let it upload videos to your account.

**How it is used.** Only to upload and publish videos you chose to post, and to check the upload status. The App does not read your messages, followers, or other content.

**Where it is stored.** Tokens are saved only on the owner's own computer, in a local file. No servers, databases, or third parties receive them.

**Sharing.** No data is sold, shared, or used for advertising or analytics.

**Retention and deletion.** Tokens are kept until you disconnect. You can revoke access at any time in TikTok under Settings > Security > Manage app permissions, after which the stored tokens stop working and are deleted.

**Children.** The App is not intended for anyone under 13.

**Changes.** This policy may be updated. The date above shows the latest version.
